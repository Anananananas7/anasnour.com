about the artist.

Anas Nour is a photographer and Manara School student based in El Gouna.

For the past two years, his work has documented labor and everyday life across Upper Egypt, from the fields of Esna and Edfu to the streets and river of Luxor, with a focus on people at work.

Recent recognition includes the People’s Choice Award at the NADIM Foundation 2025 Photography Competition, a youth award at the Zayed International Photography Competition, and a place among the winners of the group exhibition FOUND-IN-LAND at Cairo Design Week 2025. Labor of Love is his first solo exhibition.
